The Thursday discussion is a 50-minute problem-solving workshop built around three recurring formats.

### 1. Guided problem solving

Work through two or three carefully selected problems, first individually or in small groups and then as a class. Emphasize reusable solution strategies and mathematical justification.

### 2. Homework preparation and error clinic

Before a homework deadline, practice related problems without reproducing assigned questions. After grading, diagnose and repair common errors using anonymous examples.

### 3. Exam-style retrieval practice

Complete a short closed-note problem, compare approaches with a partner, and finish with a concise instructor solution.

### Default 50-minute plan

- **5 minutes:** Recap the week's key definitions and results
- **30 minutes:** Guided individual or group problem solving
- **10 minutes:** Exam-style retrieval problem
- **5 minutes:** Summary and takeaways
